# The 22-section enterprise audit framework

The full deliverable. Produce sections in this order. Every section that asks for a
score uses `X/10` plus a one-line justification — a score with no reason is noise.

---

### 1. Executive Summary
Overall impression · biggest strengths · biggest weaknesses · overall recommendation.
Write this **last**, from the findings. Lead with the single most expensive problem.

### 2. First Impression (5-Second Test)
Answer as a first-time visitor who has not read the code: What do you think this company
does? Does it feel premium / trustworthy / expensive / enterprise-ready? Would you stay
or leave, and why? → **Score /10**

### 3. Target Audience Audit
Who is the site *actually* speaking to (startup founders · SMEs · CEOs · CTOs · ops
managers · enterprises · local businesses)? Is that who it *should* speak to? Is the
messaging aligned, or hedging across everyone at once? List improvements.

### 4. Hero Section Audit
Headline · subheadline · CTA · visual hierarchy · typography · spacing · imagery ·
trust · emotion · business value · scrolling motivation.
Then: *Would a CEO understand the company in 5 seconds? Would anyone know why to choose
Drox Dev over the next firm?* **Rewrite the hero in full** — headline, subhead, both CTA
labels — do not merely describe what's wrong.

### 5. Navigation Audit
Labels · menu structure · CTA visibility · footer · mobile nav. Flag orphaned routes and
routes in nav that lead nowhere useful. **Propose a replacement nav hierarchy.**

### 6. Information Architecture
Does the page tell a story, or is it stacked sections? Map the current section order,
then give an **improved order with the reasoning for each move**. The test: does each
section answer the question the previous section raised in the buyer's head?

### 7. UX Audit — rate each /10
Typography · color system · accessibility · white space · layout · grid · hierarchy ·
readability · cards · buttons · forms · icons · images · responsiveness · interaction
feedback · mobile usability.

### 8. UI Design Audit — rate each /10
Benchmark against **Stripe, Linear, Vercel, Framer, Notion, Supabase, Cursor**. Rate
visual polish · consistency · design maturity · component quality · premium appearance ·
design system. Be specific about *what those sites do* that this one doesn't.

### 9. Copywriting Audit
Section by section: clarity · authority · trust · persuasiveness · emotion · business
value · differentiation. **Rewrite every weak block.** Ban generic agency language
("cutting-edge", "seamless", "we're passionate about", "transform your business").

### 10. Psychology Audit
Trust · curiosity · confidence · urgency · desire · safety · status · authority · social
proof · risk reduction. Name **every missing trigger** and where to place it.

### 11. Enterprise Trust Audit
Stand in front of a $250,000 approval. Would you sign? Inventory what's missing: client
logos · certifications · security posture · compliance · team credibility · office proof ·
delivery timeline · company story · awards · media · testimonials · case studies ·
metrics. Distinguish **absent** from **present-but-unconvincing** — an unnamed
testimonial is worse than no testimonial.

### 12. Conversion Audit (CRO)
CTA strategy · placement · button copy · lead generation · contact forms · friction ·
offer clarity · value proposition · lead qualification.
Predict: bounce reasons · heatmap behaviour · scroll behaviour · dead zones · drop-off
points. Label predictions as predictions.

### 13. Sales Funnel Audit
Visitor → Interest → Trust → Inquiry → Meeting → Proposal → Client.
Walk each transition and name **every friction point**, plus what's missing entirely
(no booking link is a missing stage, not a friction point).

### 14. Business Positioning
Against **Toptal, BairesDev, Thoughtbot, Netguru, ScienceSoft, Cleveroad, 10Pearls**:
why would a buyer choose them instead? What would make Drox Dev the obvious choice?
Answer honestly — the realistic wedge is usually not "we're better at everything".

### 15. Technical Audit
Performance · Core Web Vitals · accessibility · SEO · metadata · structured data ·
semantic HTML · responsive behaviour · animation performance · image optimization ·
loading speed · JavaScript quality. Delegate depth to the specialist skills.

### 16. Mobile Audit
Thumb reach · navigation · CTA visibility · readability · performance · forms. Mobile is
where most B2B first touches land — treat it as the primary experience, not the fallback.

### 17. Content Audit
Headlines · paragraphs · case studies · testimonials · FAQs · services · about page ·
contact page. **Name the content that does not exist and must**, with a spec for each.

### 18. Premium Brand Audit
Does this read as a **$500 freelancer · $10k agency · $100k agency · multi-million-dollar
engineering company**? Pick one, justify it, then list **every element** dragging the
perception down.

### 19. Enterprise Readiness
Would an enterprise shortlist Drox Dev today? If not, every reason, each with a fix.

### 20. Redesign Roadmap
Priorities 1–5, sequenced so each unblocks the next. Estimated business impact per
priority (as a labelled estimate, with reasoning).

### 21. Scorecard
Brand · UX · UI · Trust · Authority · Copywriting · Accessibility · Performance ·
Conversion · Enterprise Readiness · **Overall**. Format: `⭐⭐⭐☆☆  6/10 — one-line why`.
The Overall is a judgement, not an average — say which dimension dominated it.

### 22. Top 25 Highest-ROI Improvements
Ranked by business impact, highest first. Table:

| # | Issue | Recommendation | Expected impact | Difficulty | Priority |

Recommendations must be executable: name the file, the copy, the component. If fewer
than 25 real improvements exist, deliver fewer and say so — padding the list to 25
buries the top five.
